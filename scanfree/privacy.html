ScanFree Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

ScanFree does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

Your documents

Documents, ID cards, passports and photos you scan are stored only in the app on your device. Scanning, text recognition (OCR) and PDF creation all happen on your device. ScanFree has no server and no cloud storage, and your documents are never uploaded anywhere by the app. When you export or share a file, it goes only where you send it using the iOS share sheet.

No accounts, no tracking

ScanFree has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking. The app makes no network connections to collect data about you.

Camera and photo access

Camera access is used only to scan documents at your request. Photo library access is used only when you choose to import a photo to scan. Neither is used for any other purpose.
