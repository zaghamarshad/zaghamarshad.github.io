🔒 Privacy Policy — Sahih: Halal Food Scanner

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Sahih: Halal Food Scanner ("the app") is designed to work without collecting your personal data. This policy explains exactly what happens when you use it.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts and no sign-up. It contains no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. We do not collect, store on our servers, sell, or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your scan history, favorites, notes, and settings (including your school-of-thought choice) are stored only on your device. They are never transmitted to us. You can delete individual scans in the app, and deleting the app removes everything.

The one network request: barcode lookup

When you scan or type a barcode, the app sends only the barcode number to Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org) to fetch that product's ingredient list. No account, identity, or scan history is sent with it. Open Food Facts handles the request under its own privacy policy.

Everything else works fully on-device: checking typed ingredients, reading a label photo (the photo never leaves your device), and every verdict calculation.

Purchases

Sahih Pro is an in-app purchase processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan barcodes and ingredient labels while you use those features. No photos or video are stored or sent anywhere; label text is recognized on the device.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
