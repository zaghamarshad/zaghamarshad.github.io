Sodium Scan: Salt Food Tracker - Support

Sodium Scan helps you see the sodium (salt) hiding in restaurant, takeout and homemade meals, and track it against your daily goal. Every food in the built-in guide shows its sodium up front, so you know before you eat.

Getting started

  1. Open the app and tap Log a meal.
  2. Search for what you ate - every result shows its sodium in mg. Tap a result, set your servings, and tap Add.
  3. The ring on the Today screen shows how much of your daily goal you have left.

Star your regular meals to log them again from Favorites in two taps, or use Quick Add for anything that is not in the list. Food values come from USDA FoodData Central standard reference data, so a specific kitchen's plate will vary.
