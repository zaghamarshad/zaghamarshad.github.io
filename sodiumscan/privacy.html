Sodium Scan: Salt Food Tracker - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Data we collect

None. Sodium Scan does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Your food log

The meals you log, your favorites and your daily goal are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to any server.

Food data

Sodium values come from USDA FoodData Central standard reference data stored inside the app. Values are estimates for standard items; a specific kitchen's plate will vary. Sodium Scan is a tracking aid, not medical advice.

Purchases

Sodium Scan Pro is sold through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple processes the payment; we do not receive your payment details.
