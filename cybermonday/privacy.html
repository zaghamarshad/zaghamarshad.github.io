🔒 Privacy Policy — Cyber Monday 2026: Deals & Ads

Last updated: November 19, 2026.

What the app itself collects

The app has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect, store, or sell your personal information. Your price watchlist and settings are stored only on your device and are never uploaded.

Advertising — Google AdMob

The free version of the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure those ads, Google may use:

  • Identifiers — your device's advertising identifier.
  • Usage data — product interaction and advertising data.

This data is used for third-party advertising and may be used to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies, as described in the app's App Store privacy label. If you allow tracking when iOS asks, ads can be personalized; if you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized. Buying either one-time unlock (Remove Ads or Track Price + NO ADS) turns all ads off, and no advertising data is collected from that point on.

Google's own privacy policy describes how Google handles this data: policies.google.com/privacy.

Deal links

Tapping a deal opens the retailer's website in Safari. What you do on a retailer's site is covered by that retailer's own privacy policy. This app is not affiliated with the retailers listed.

Children

The app is a shopping utility intended for adults. It does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
