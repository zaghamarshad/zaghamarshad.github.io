❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is the app free?

A: Yes. The app is free to download and use, supported by ads. Every deal, the countdown, and all categories are free for everyone.

Q: How do I remove the ads?

A: With a one-time purchase — never a subscription. Remove Ads ($3.99) turns off every ad in the app, forever. Track Price + NO ADS ($5.99) removes ads and adds the price watchlist: save a deal, set a target price, and see when it drops. Either purchase removes all ads.

Q: I bought Remove Ads before. How do I restore it?

A: Open Settings in the app, or the unlock screen, and tap "Reprint my receipt — Restore Purchases" while signed in with the same Apple ID. Your purchase comes back at no charge — you never pay twice.

Q: How often are the deals updated?

A: The deals feed is curated by hand and refreshed daily through Cyber Week, from November 27 to December 2, 2026. The app also carries a built-in copy of the feed, so it works offline.

Q: Why does tapping a deal open Safari?

A: Deal links open the retailer's own website in Safari, where you can check the live price and buy. This app does not sell anything itself and is not affiliated with the retailers listed.

Q: How does the price watchlist work?

A: With Track Price + NO ADS, tap CLIP on any deal to save it, set the price you want to pay, and the app shows Watching, Dropped, or Target Hit as the feed updates. Your watchlist stays on your device — there are no accounts and nothing is uploaded.

Q: How do I get a refund for an unlock?

A: Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

🔒 Privacy

The app does not collect personal information and has no accounts. Your watchlist is stored only on your device. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which uses your device identifier and usage data for third-party advertising, as described in the App Store privacy label. Read the full Privacy Policy.
