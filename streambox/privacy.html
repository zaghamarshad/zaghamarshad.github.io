StreamBox IPTV Player - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Data we collect

None. StreamBox does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Your playlists and logins

The playlist links and provider login details you add are stored only on your device. Your password is kept in the iOS Keychain. These details are sent only to the playlist or provider server you entered, so the app can load your channels - they are never sent to us.

Playback

Video streams play directly between your device and the source in your playlist. We do not operate servers and cannot see what you watch.

Purchases

StreamBox Pro is sold through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple processes the payment; we do not receive your payment details.
