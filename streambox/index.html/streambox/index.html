StreamBox IPTV Player

StreamBox is a simple IPTV player for iPhone. You bring your own playlist or provider login - StreamBox organises it into a clean, fast TV experience.

Getting started

  1. Open the app and tap Add a Playlist.
  2. Choose Playlist Link and paste your M3U link, or choose Provider Login and enter the server, username and password your provider gave you.
  3. Your channels appear under Live TV. Tap any channel to watch.

No playlist yet? Tap Try Demo in the Add a Playlist screen to load free public test channels.

StreamBox Pro

Pro is a one-time purchase (no subscription). It unlocks unlimited playlists and the full TV guide with what's on now and next.
