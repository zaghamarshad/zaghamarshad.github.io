Common questions

  • Where are my photos? Only in the app, on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere.
  • What is the open-jar number on my products? That is the period after opening (PAO) — how many months a product is meant to be used once opened. Skincare Pro tracks it for you.
  • Is Pro a subscription? No. Pro is a one-time purchase. Pay once, keep it.
  • Does the app analyze my skin? No. It is a diary, not a dermatologist — it never scores, diagnoses, or judges your skin.
