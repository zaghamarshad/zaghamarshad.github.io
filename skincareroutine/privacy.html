Privacy Policy

Skincare Routine & Photo Log — last updated November 5, 2026

The short version

Data Not Collected. This app does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything you put into it — your routines, your progress photos, your product cabinet, and your skin diary — stays on your device, in the app's own storage.

What stays on your device

  • Your routine checklists and check-ins
  • Your progress photos (stored in the app's private folder, with complete file protection)
  • Your product cabinet and diary entries
  • Your settings, including reminder times

None of this is sent to us or to any server we run. The app has no account system and no analytics.

Camera

The camera is used only when you tap to take a progress photo, and only inside the app. Photos the app takes are saved only in the app's own storage on your device.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing about them leaves the device.

Purchases

Skincare Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Your device backup

Your app data is included in your own device backup (iCloud or computer) if you have backup turned on. That backup is yours, under your Apple account.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time from inside the app: Settings → Delete all data. Deleting the app also removes its data from your device.
