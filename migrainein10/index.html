Migraine in 10

Headache Diary — support

Migraine in 10 is a personal headache diary built for one moment: logging an attack while it is happening. One tap starts a timer, a giant pain scale and a single tap for medication do the rest, and the diary can be exported as a one-page PDF for a clinician visit.

Getting help

Email us any time at reskinmania@gmail.com. We read everything and reply as soon as we can.

Common questions

  • Where is my data? On your device, only. There is no account and no server. Deleting the app deletes the diary.
  • How do I share my diary with my doctor? Open the Trends tab and export the one-page PDF, then share or print it. PDF export is the one feature unlocked by Migraine in 10 Pro (a one-time purchase).
  • How do I restore Pro? Settings → “Restore a previous purchase”, signed in with the same Apple ID used to buy it.
  • Can I log an attack from the Lock Screen? Yes — add the Migraine in 10 widget to your Lock Screen and tap it to start logging.

Migraine in 10 is a personal record. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition and is not medical advice. If a headache is sudden and severe, or unlike anything you have felt before, seek medical care promptly.
