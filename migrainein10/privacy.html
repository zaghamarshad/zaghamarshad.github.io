Privacy Policy

Migraine in 10: Headache Diary · Last updated October 29, 2026

The short version

We collect no data. None. Migraine in 10 has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. Everything you log stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your attack diary (times, pain levels, medication names you type, trigger tags, notes) is stored in a file on your device, in the app's private storage shared only with the app's own widget.
  • Voice notes you record are audio files in the same on-device storage. They are never uploaded anywhere.
  • The widget reads only the current attack state (start time and pain level) and a weekly count, from the same on-device storage.

Leaving the device — only when you choose

The one way information leaves your device is the PDF report: the app creates it on-device, and it goes only where you send it through the iOS share sheet (for example, to your doctor). We never see it.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when you tap record for a voice note, and only for that recording.

Purchases

The optional Migraine in 10 Pro unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment information.

Deleting your data

Delete attacks inside the app (History, or Settings → Delete all logged attacks), or delete the app to remove everything.
