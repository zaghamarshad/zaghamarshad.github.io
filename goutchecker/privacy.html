Gout Checker: Uric Acid Foods - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Data we collect

None. Gout Checker does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Your logs

The foods you check and the flare-ups you log are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to any server.

Food data

Purine values come from published nutrition research (Kaneko et al., 2014) stored inside the app. Values are reference estimates per 100 g; individual foods vary. Gout Checker is a food reference tool, not medical advice.

Purchases

Gout Checker Pro is sold through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple processes the payment; we do not receive your payment details.
