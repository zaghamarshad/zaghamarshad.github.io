Gout Checker: Uric Acid Foods - Support

Gout Checker puts the purine content of 244 foods and drinks in your pocket. Purines are printed on no food label, so every food in the built-in database shows its purine mg per 100 g with a clear band: low, moderate, high, or very high.

Getting started

  • Open the app and tap Check Food.
  • Search for a food or browse by category. Every result shows its purine value up front.
  • Tap a food, set your portion, and see the purine load for the serving you actually eat.
  • Log flare-ups in the Flares tab. The app reviews what you checked in the 48 hours before a flare and surfaces your likely triggers.

Food values are compiled from published nutrition research (Kaneko et al., 2014) and stored inside the app. Gout Checker is a food reference tool. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition and is not a substitute for medical advice.
