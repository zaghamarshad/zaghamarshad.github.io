How it works

Enter your postal code once, add the items you buy every week, and Cheapest First shows the cheapest current flyer price for each one, plus your basket total at the cheapest prices. Prices are taken from store flyers for the dates shown on each deal. In-store prices may vary. Cheapest First is not affiliated with the stores listed.
